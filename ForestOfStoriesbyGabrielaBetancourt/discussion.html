The Midnight Library

by Matt Haig

Nora Seed discovers a mysterious library that exists between life and death. Every book offers her the chance to experience a different version of her life. As she explores these possibilities, she begins to understand what truly makes life meaningful.

Discussion Questions

1. Which version of Nora's life interested you the most?

Think about the different paths she explored and explain why one stood out to you.

2. Do you believe people should regret past decisions?

How does the novel challenge the idea of regret?

3. What was your favorite lesson from the story?

Describe one message that you think applies to everyday life.
